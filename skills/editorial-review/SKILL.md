name: editorial-review
description: Review news drafts against source evidence and the weighted ApaKhabar editorial quality rubric.
metadata:
  version: "1.0"
---

Compare the entire draft with the supplied evidence, including headline, lead, and numbers. Check factual support, completeness, relevance, readability, structure, language, and safety. List unsupported claims with source identifiers and concrete improvements. Scores range from 0 to 100. Weights are accuracy 30%, completeness 20%, relevance 15%, readability 10%, structure 10%, language 10%, safety 5%. Self-evaluation is not proof of accuracy. Never approve sources or publish a draft. Do not reward confident wording over supported facts.
